## Text

Governing a great state is like cooking small fish.

Let the kingdom be governed according to the Tao, and the manes of the departed will not manifest their spiritual energy.  It is not that those manes have not that spiritual energy, but it will not be employed to hurt men.  It is not that it could not hurt men, but neither does the ruling sage hurt them.

When these two do not injuriously affect each other, their good influences converge in the virtue (of the Tao).

## Reflection

Govern a large group the way you would cook a small fish: gently, without poking it. Turn it too often and it falls apart. Constant reorganizing, shifting priorities and tinkering exhaust a group and break its cohesion. Steadiness is a gift a leader gives. Make fewer, better interventions, then leave them alone long enough to work.

## Question

What have I been stirring that needs to be left alone to cook?

## Practice

Before your next change of plan, reorg or new initiative, wait a week. If it still matters after that, explain why it is worth the disturbance.
